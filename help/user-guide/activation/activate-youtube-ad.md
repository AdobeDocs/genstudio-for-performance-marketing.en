# Activate a YouTube ad

Adobe GenStudio for Performance Marketing supports the activation of YouTube ad experiences through Google Ads Demand Gen campaigns.

**Supported formats**: Shorts.

Activating a YouTube ad follows the [same general steps](create-activation.md) required for activation to other paid ad channels. This page covers YouTube-specific prerequisites and setup fields. At this time, GenStudio for Performance Marketing supports Demand Gen campaign setup only.

GenStudio system managers and editors can activate ad experiences.

## Prerequisites

* A Google Ads account with a Demand Gen campaign already configured. GenStudio for Performance Marketing does not create Demand Gen campaigns.
* The target ad group must already exist in that Demand Gen campaign.

## Connect your Google Ads account

Before your organization can activate experiences, a GenStudio system manager must connect your Google Ads account to GenStudio for Performance Marketing. Go to **[!UICONTROL More]** > **[!UICONTROL Settings]** > **[!UICONTROL Google Ads]** > **[!UICONTROL Connect]**.

## YouTube setup fields

Approved video assets and description copy are locked and can't be edited during activation, since they already went through review and approval in [!DNL Content]. You can edit:

* **Text fields**: Call-to-action, Business name, Destination URL, URL Parameters, Tracking ID (used as the platform ad name)
* **Platform setup fields**: Account, Campaign, Ad Group, Logo

Select the logo from the logos available in your connected Google Ads account.
